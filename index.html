Hi, my name is JoAnne Gomez. I'm a Registered Nurse with more than 27 years of critical care experience in Toronto. I spent almost seven years in the Medical-Surgical ICU at Toronto Western Hospital and twelve years at the Trauma-Neuro ICU in St. Mike's Hospital. I've also taken ICU shifts at Sunnybrook and North York General, and I've worked as a travel ICU nurse in New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Grand Cayman Island. I've cared for patients with major trauma, brain injuries and complex surgeries. Since 2012, I've been a post-operative recovery nurse at the Toronto Cosmetic Surgery Institute. I hold a Critical Care Certificate from Humber College and a degree in Psychology from the University of Nebraska, which helps me connect with patients and families when they need reassurance. I learn new systems fast, I work well with any team, and I'm ready to bring this experience to your work environment. Thank you for watching. I'd love to talk with you.
